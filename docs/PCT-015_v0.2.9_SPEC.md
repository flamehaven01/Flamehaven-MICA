# PCT-015 v0.2.9 Spec

## Purpose

`PCT-015` enforces the promotion provenance boundary.
It answers:

> Do approved lessons and bound invariant evidence cite real, promotion-safe source evidence?

## Scope

Applies to:

- `mica.candidates.json`
- promoted lesson records derived from candidates
- bound invariant evidence derived from candidates

## Inputs

- `mica.candidates.json`
- `memory/mica.observe.jsonl`
- archive / playbook artifacts when present

## Minimum PASS conditions

- every approved lesson candidate cites at least one valid `source_event_id`
- every bound invariant evidence candidate cites `origin_episode`
- every bound invariant evidence candidate cites `supporting_event_ids`
- cited source events exist in the observation stream or valid tombstone set
- Stage 2 and Stage 3 objects carry review provenance
- Stage 3 objects do not originate from `opaque` trust-tier evidence

## FAIL conditions

- approved lesson without source-event trace
- bound invariant evidence without `origin_episode`
- bound invariant evidence without `supporting_event_ids`
- cited source event does not exist
- cited source event identity was GC-deleted without tombstone preservation
- `operator_review.state` not approved on a Stage 2 or Stage 3 artifact
- Stage 3 artifact depends solely on `opaque` trust-tier evidence

## Validation notes

- `source_event_ids` prove evidentiary lineage.
- `origin_episode` explains the grounding narrative that archive-grade truth points back to.
- `supporting_event_ids` are stronger than a single opaque summary because they preserve audit replayability.
